The problem

Liquidity providers have capital. They cannot borrow against it.

Today

Principal is parked

BTC and MUSD inside a concentrated-liquidity position earn fees and rewards, and do nothing else.

The cost

Credit means exiting

A staked position is an NFT in a gauge. A lending market cannot take it, so an LP who needs MUSD has to pull liquidity.

Hock

Shares a market accepts

One pooled position, split into ERC-20 shares that Morpho on Mezo takes as collateral.

Who it is for: Mezo BTC/MUSD liquidity providers who want MUSD credit. Demand is a hypothesis. No outside user has used Hock yet.

How it works

Deposit once. Your position becomes collateral.

01 DEPOSIT

BTC + MUSD

HockVault adds liquidity on a fixed wide range and stakes it in the Mezo gauge.

02 SHARES

HOCK tokens

Depositors get ERC-20 shares of the pooled position. Unused tokens are refunded exactly.

03 LOCK

Morpho market

Shares are posted as collateral in a MUSD market at 77% LLTV.

04 BORROW

MUSD out

Repay any time. Exit returns the BTC and MUSD.

Built on Mezo's own pieces: canonical MUSD, Mezo Pools, the CL gauge and the Mezo Morpho deployment.

HOCK04 / 08
Safety

Priced by feeds, never by the pool's spot price.

What the oracle does

  • Share value = BTC/USD divided by MUSD/USD. MUSD is never assumed to be one dollar.
  • A zero, future-dated or 24-hour-old price reverts the valuation.
  • Deposits and harvests revert if spot drifts more than 5% from the fair price.

What we disclose

  • On testnet the MUSD/USD price is our own labelled stand-in feed, because Mezo's testnet Pyth feed is stale.
  • No TWAP check yet. Owner is keeper and treasury, with no timelock.
  • Collateral is an LP position, so it carries impermanent loss. Not audited.
